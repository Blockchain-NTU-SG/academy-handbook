# Beginner content review

> **Read as a learner, not as an editor.**

This guide is for Education subcommittee read-throughs of the Foundation. The
goal is to find where understanding breaks, not to make every sentence sound
more polished.

## Suggested process

1. Read the assigned Week and Parts in order.
2. Do not edit while reading.
3. Record the exact page and section where confusion appears.
4. Explain what you thought the passage meant.
5. Classify the friction below.
6. Optionally say what might have helped; you do not need to propose a rewrite.

Education leadership decides the actual fix. More content is not automatically
better: the problem may be sequencing, a missing prerequisite, a misplaced
example or an optional detail that should be easier to skip.

## Feedback categories

### Blocker

You cannot continue or complete the task without clarification.

### Concept gap

You can continue, but the mental model is likely incomplete or wrong.

### Friction

The idea is eventually understandable, but it is unnecessarily difficult or
requires repeated rereading.

### Nice-to-have

The page could feel smoother, but the issue does not materially affect
understanding.

## What to record

- page, heading or step;
- your background and what you expected to know;
- the exact phrase, example or transition that caused trouble;
- what you thought it meant;
- the category and whether the issue blocked the task.

You do not need to propose a solution. Identifying where understanding broke is
useful by itself.

## Week 3 technical read-through

Where practical, follow the actual Remix/wallet/read/write flow rather than only
reading the prose. Record any point where the handbook assumes knowledge or UI
familiarity you did not have.

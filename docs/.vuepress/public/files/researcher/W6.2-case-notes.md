## Available evidence

- F01–F13 are fictional chain observations; swap rows consolidate a call and
  its result. Do not count them again as additional token logs.
- F14–F16 are fictional LP ownership and contract-state observations.
- F17 is a website claim. F18 is an unverified provider label plus a
  limited-history observation; neither establishes ownership.
- The window is bounded. First observed is not necessarily first ever.
  Recipient history outside the window, private service records and admin
  ownership evidence are not supplied. Missing evidence remains unknown.

## Questions to investigate

What could explain an equal split followed by similar swaps? Does funding
add independent evidence of control or admit a service explanation? What
distinguishes distribution/market-making from a coordinated sell programme?
Does position ownership and authority establish the lock claim? Where does
public evidence stop across chains and a custodial deposit?

These are research questions, not a concealed verdict. Use the lesson's
observation / interpretation / alternative / confidence / next-check table.
No real actor is accused, and no model answer is included.

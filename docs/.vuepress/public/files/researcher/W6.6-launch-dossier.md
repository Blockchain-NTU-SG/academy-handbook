# Synthetic launch dossier — independent investigation

Original Academy case, created 26 October 2026. NOVEL, Case-L1, addresses
A–D, Sponsor-S, Market-M and Locker-L are invented aliases. There are no
real transaction hashes or people behind these records. Units are display
units. This is a bounded dataset, not a claim about a real project.

## System and public claims

NOVEL is a fictional governance token for a data-service protocol. The
website claims “community-led ownership” and “locked liquidity”. Governance
can allocate treasury funds; no direct token-holder fee distribution is
specified. No implementation/admin records for the token or locker are supplied.

USDQ is an invented dollar-denominated quote unit, not a real stablecoin.
N03 reports six swaps totalling 3m NOVEL. N04 reflects that reduction from
the initial 20m; it does not establish how much belongs to independent people.

Use the observations to choose a bounded question. Possible measurements
include share of reported circulation, observed sold/allocated share or
volume concentration, with explicit denominator choices. No particular
conclusion or calculation is prescribed.

The missing history, label provenance, admin powers, attribution and private
allocation terms remain unknown. A coherent operational-distribution story
and a coordinated-control story may both fit some observations. Identify
the next evidence that would distinguish them.

This dossier contains the case's evidence: its records are synthetic and
cannot establish facts about a real protocol or person. No separate real-chain
field check is required for this default exercise. A learner choosing a real
case instead must use direct chain evidence relevant to that case's question.

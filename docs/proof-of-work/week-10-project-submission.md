# Week 10 — Project Submission

Turn the W9 brief into a final reviewable project. The project should make it
possible for another person to inspect what you attempted, understand the
evidence or implementation, and see its limitations.

The final form depends on your track and project. For example:

| Track | Possible reviewable forms |
|---|---|
| **Builder** | A small working application or integration, a repository, a usable demo, and a README explaining setup, verification and known limits |
| **Researcher** | An article or report, reproducible analysis or dataset, sources and method, evidence supporting a bounded claim, and stated limitations |

These are examples, not a requirement to produce every item in a row. Agree
with the Education Team on a form appropriate to the W9 scope and available
evidence.

## Final review

Submit the artefact and the evidence needed to inspect it. Explain any material
change from the W9 brief, what you verified, and what remains uncertain or out
of scope. A polished appearance cannot replace a working verification path or
well-supported research claim.

## Completion

This milestone is worth **200 points**. Complete means another person can
inspect the project, understand what was attempted, and distinguish demonstrated
results from limitations. Work that is incomplete or materially unsupported is
returned for revision under the Academy's binary review model.

# Researcher Deep Dive

Researcher learners develop the transferable skill of **decomposing unfamiliar
Web3 systems**: reconstructing how a system works, measuring it with evidence,
and making an evidence-based claim about its risks and behaviour. The connected
capabilities are system research (how it works), on-chain investigation (what
happened), and economic research (what the evidence means).

This is cross-domain Web3 systems research, not a DeFi-only track. Bitcoin,
rollups, Uniswap, lending and other protocols are training cases that support
methods learners can later apply to NFTs, stablecoins, real-world assets
(RWA), prediction markets, DePIN, AI × Web3, infrastructure, governance or
future domains.

The track selected at the end of Week 4 remains the learner's capability path
through W10. The application domain, research question and project idea can
evolve through W5–W8; W9 is when the learner scopes a realistic Project Brief
and receives feedback.

## Weeks

- [Week 5 · Deconstruct Blockchain Systems](./week-5/)
- [Week 6 · Protocol Economics and System Analysis](./week-6/)
- [Week 7 · On-Chain Evidence and Measurement](./week-7/)
- [Week 8 · Valuation, Risk and Investigation](./week-8/)

Each week contains three Part tasks. Every Part task is worth **100 points**
(300 per week) and is assessed by completion and understanding, with revision
when the work is incomplete or materially incorrect. There is no separate
weekly submission.

> measurement → valuation, risk and a bounded conclusion. Current required
> authoring estimates are W5 2 h 5 min, W6 2 h 10 min, W7 2 h 20 min and W8

# Researcher Week 8 — Valuation, Risk and Investigation

Connect token and liquidity evidence to meaningful valuation comparisons,
identify dependencies and governance powers, then produce an independent,
bounded investigation brief.

Each Part has one reviewable task worth **100 points**. There is no separate
weekly submission.

| Part | Title | Estimated total | Task status |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Token, Liquidity and Valuation Evidence](./part-1-token-and-market-evidence.md) | 60 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 2 | [Dependency, Governance and Risk](./part-2-dependency-governance-and-risk.md) | 50 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 3 | [Independent Investigation Brief](./part-3-evidence-to-claim.md) | 50 min | Drafting |

**Required estimate: 160 minutes (2 h 40 min).** These are approximate learner-time estimates, not deadlines; revise them as learner evidence improves. Optional exploration is outside the estimate.

**Assessment:** 3 Part tasks × 100 points = **300 points** in the Academy progression. Academy participants submit each Part for a **Complete** or **Revise** review. Independent readers may use the criteria to self-check without formal point tracking. There are no partial scores.

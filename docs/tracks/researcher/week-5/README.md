# Researcher Week 5 — Deconstruct Blockchain Systems

Reconstruct an unfamiliar system from its primary documents, trust architecture
and economic mechanism. Examples are training cases, not permanent domains.

Each Part has one reviewable task worth **100 points**. There is no separate
weekly submission.

| Part | Title | Estimated total | Task status |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Whitepaper → System Model](./part-1-whitepaper-to-system-model.md) | 40 min | Drafting |
| 2 | [Architecture and Trust Reconstruction](./part-2-architecture-and-trust.md) | 45 min | Drafting |
| 3 | [Mechanism and Market Design](./part-3-mechanism-and-market-design.md) | 40 min | Drafting |

**Required estimate: 125 minutes (2 h 5 min).** These are authoring estimates
for pilot review, not deadlines. Optional exploration is outside the estimate.

**Assessment:** 3 Part tasks × 100 points = **300 points**. Completion is
binary; incomplete or materially incorrect work is returned for revision.

# Researcher Week 5 — Deconstruct Blockchain Systems

This week teaches learners to reconstruct an unfamiliar system from its
documents, architecture, mechanisms, incentives and failure paths. The examples
are training cases, not permanent domains.

Each Part has one lightweight, reviewable hands-on task worth **100 points**.

**There is no separate weekly submission.** These six Part tasks are the
assessed progress evidence for this week.

| Part | Title | Estimated total | Task status |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Whitepaper → System Model](./part-1-whitepaper-to-system-model.md) | 40 min | Drafting |
| 2 | [Architecture and Trust Reconstruction](./part-2-architecture-and-trust.md) | 45 min | Drafting |
| 3 | [Mechanism and Market Design](./part-3-mechanism-and-market-design.md) | 40 min | Drafting |
| 4 | [Collateral, Credit and DeFi Economics](./part-4-collateral-credit-and-risk.md) | 40 min | Drafting |
| 5 | [Token and Incentive Economics](./part-5-token-and-incentive-economics.md) | 45 min | Drafting |
| 6 | [Independent System Analysis](./part-6-system-analysis.md) | 45 min | Drafting |

**Required estimate: 255 minutes (4 h 15 min)**, including hands-on work.
Read → reconstruct → calculate → model credit → analyse incentives →
independently decompose a system. Optional exploration is outside that estimate.

**Assessment:** 6 Part tasks × 100 points = **600 points**. Completion is
binary; incomplete or materially incorrect work is returned for revision.

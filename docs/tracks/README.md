# Deep Dive tracks

After the shared Foundation, the full Academy offers two Deep Dive tracks. At
the end of Week 4, each learner selects Builder or Researcher. The selected
track remains the learner's capability path through W10. The domain, project
topic, research question and final deliverable can evolve during W5–W8; W9 is
when the Project Brief and feedback session set a realistic final scope.

| Track | Positioning | Deep Dive |
|---|---|---|
| [Builder](./builder/) | Learn to **compose unfamiliar Web3 systems** | W5 programmatic development → W6 app integration → W7 reliable engineering → W8 integration and shipping |
| [Researcher](./researcher/) | Learn to **decompose unfamiliar Web3 systems** | W5 system reconstruction → W6 protocol economics → W7 on-chain evidence and measurement → W8 valuation and risk |

Data/querying and product thinking are transferable methods or application
lenses, not separate Academy tracks. Domains such as DeFi, stablecoins, NFTs,
RWA, prediction markets, DePIN, infrastructure, governance and AI × Web3 are
training cases that may appear in either track.

Each Deep Dive week has three Part tasks worth 100 points each, for 300 points
per week. There is no separate weekly submission. Both tracks retain their
approved 12-Part sequence; individual pages retain their draft/review status.

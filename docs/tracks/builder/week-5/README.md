# Builder Week 5 — Programmatic Web3 Development

This week moves from guided Remix interaction to a real development environment
and programmatic composition. It builds on, rather than repeats, Foundation's
Solidity, ABI and RPC introduction.

You work in the
[Builder starter](https://github.com/Blockchain-NTU-SG/academy-builder-starter)
all week: run it, extend its contract, operate that contract from a script,
connect a wallet in the browser, turn events into an activity list, and finish
with one small app that works end to end on Sepolia.

Each Part has one lightweight, reviewable hands-on task worth **100 points**.

**There is no separate weekly submission.** These six Part tasks are the
assessed progress evidence for this week.

| Part | Planned title | Estimated total | Task status |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Run the Stack](./part-1-run-the-stack.md) | 35 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 2 | [Contract Engineering](./part-2-contract-engineering.md) | 45 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 3 | [Programmatic Contract Interaction](./part-3-programmatic-contract-interaction.md) | 45 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 4 | [Wallet and Frontend Integration](./part-4-wallet-and-frontend-integration.md) | 50 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 5 | [Events as Data](./part-5-events-as-data.md) | 40 min hands-on | Drafting |
| 6 | [End-to-End Vertical Slice](./part-6-end-to-end-vertical-slice.md) | 75 min hands-on | Drafting |

**Required estimate: 290 minutes (4 h 50 min)**, including hands-on work.
These are authoring estimates to test in the pilot, not completion deadlines.
Optional exploration is outside the estimate.

**Assessment:** 6 Part tasks × 100 points = **600 points**. Completion is
binary; incomplete or materially incorrect work is returned for revision.

# Builder Deep Dive

Builder learners develop the transferable skill of **composing unfamiliar Web3
systems**: turning a clear behaviour into contracts, programmatic interactions,
interfaces and reliable evidence.

This Deep Dive begins after Foundation's Solidity, Remix, ABI and RPC
introduction. It is not another smart-contract introduction. No programming
background beyond Foundation is assumed; Week 5 Part 1 introduces the local
development tools and terminal workflow before the later Parts use them.

Builder is the selected capability path for W5–W8. W9–W10 Project Work applies
the Builder capability developed in the Deep Dive. The application domain and
project idea can evolve through W5–W8; W9 is when the learner scopes a realistic
Project Brief and receives feedback.

## Weeks

- [Week 5 · Programmatic Web3 Development](./week-5/)
- [Week 6 · Web3 App Integration](./week-6/)
- [Week 7 · Reliable Web3 Engineering](./week-7/)
- [Week 8 · Integration, Shipping and Independence](./week-8/)

Each week contains three Part tasks. Every Part task is worth **100 points**
(300 per week) and is assessed by completion and understanding, with revision
when the work is incomplete or materially incorrect. There is no separate
weekly submission.

> **Estimated learner workload:** Required work is estimated at W5 2 h 5 min,
> W6 2 h 45 min, W7 2 h 15 min and W8 4 h 15 min. These are approximate times,
> not deadlines. Optional exploration is not assessed, and weeks are not
> expected to take equal time. Estimates may be revised as learner evidence
> improves.

All twelve Deep Dive Parts are organised across W5–W8. Individual lessons
retain their repository authoring status; workload estimates are approximate.

# Learn independently or join the Academy

The Handbook is the complete public curriculum. You can follow W0 through W10
independently, whether or not you take part in the organised Blockchain@NTU
Academy experience.

## Independent learning

- Read and follow the full curriculum at your own pace.
- Use the published task and completion criteria to guide your work.
- Self-check, or invite a peer, mentor or educator to give feedback.
- No Academy account, NTU affiliation or organised participation is needed.

## Blockchain@NTU Academy participation

The organised Academy experience uses the same curriculum. Through Web3 Career
Build (WCB), it can add structured pacing, official task submission and review,
and progress and points tracking. These participation features do not change
the Handbook's lesson content, tasks, completion criteria or project requirements.

Use the official WCB entry point below for participation details and
availability. If it shows no current offering, the independent W0–W10 path
remains available; WCB does not control access to the curriculum.

[View Blockchain@NTU Academy on WCB](https://web3career.build/en/programs/blockchain-ntu-academy)

# Programme overview

Blockchain@NTU Academy follows one continuous story:

This public, self-paced curriculum is complete from Week 0 through Week 10.
Follow it independently in order, or use the stage links to revisit a topic.

| Stage | Focus | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Week 0 | Entering Web3 safely | A safe vocabulary and tool map |
| Week 1 | Web3, blockchain, crypto assets and wallets | First-principles foundation |
| Week 2 | Blockchain architectures, Ethereum and interoperability | Architecture and risk map |
| Week 3 | Smart contracts, tokens and DApps | Contract and application foundations |
| Week 4 | Web3 industry, research and contribution | Track Selection Card |
| Weeks 5–8 | Builder or Researcher Deep Dive | 3 Part tasks per week × 100 points |
| Week 9 | Project Brief + Feedback Session | Scoped, feedback-informed brief · 200 points |
| Week 10 | Project Submission | Final reviewable project · 200 points |

The curriculum has two Deep Dive tracks: **Builder**, who learns to compose
unfamiliar Web3 systems, and **Researcher**, who learns to decompose them. At
the end of Week 4, the learner selects one track and stays on that capability
path through W10. The domain, research question and project deliverable may
evolve during W5–W8; W9 is where the learner scopes the project brief after
feedback.
Domains such as DeFi, stablecoins, NFTs, RWA, prediction markets, DePIN,
infrastructure, governance and AI × Web3 are cases and application lenses, not
separate tracks.

## Scoring at a glance

| Stage | Completion-based points |
|---|---:|
| Foundation W1–W4 | 4 Anchor Missions × 100 = **400** |
| W5–W8 Deep Dive | 4 weeks × 3 Part tasks × 100 = **1,200** |
| W9 Project Brief + Feedback Session | **200** |
| W10 Project Submission | **200** |
| **Total** | **2,000** |

W0 is onboarding and does not add points. Foundation keeps assessment light
through one Anchor Mission per week. Deep Dive remains lightweight but becomes
more hands-on: each Part contains one small completion-based task embedded
directly in the learning, with no separate weekly submission. A task is
completed or returned for revision; there is no partial-score rubric. If work
does not yet meet the completion criteria, revise it and check again.

## How to read the handbook

- **Core** means you must be able to explain or demonstrate it.
- **Landscape** means you should recognise it and roughly explain its place.
- **Further Exploration** is optional depth.

Foundation follows richer content, lighter assessment. Deep Dive keeps tasks
embedded in the learning rather than adding extra homework. Weekly pacing is a
recommended rhythm, not a deadline. Any required activity that sends a
transaction, deploys a contract, performs a state-changing wallet action, or
otherwise requires funds or an on-chain signature is testnet-only; some lessons
may ask you to inspect public mainnet data read-only. Never use real funds for
any learning activity.

## Start learning

Begin with [Week 0](./), then continue through the shared [Foundation](../foundation/).
After Week 4, choose Builder or Researcher, complete Weeks 5–8, and use the
W9/W10 project criteria to scope and present a reviewable project. You can
self-check each task or ask someone you trust to review it; no separate account
or programme access is needed.
